🗞️ About this wire

How The Dev Desk tracks the news

This page is the live research wire behind The Dev Desk show. It watches the places developer news actually happens — GitHub releases and trending repos, the Hacker News front page, the dev.to community, and the status pages of major platforms — and lays them out like a newsroom rundown.

Every item links to its primary source. Stories are verified against the original release, post, or status page before they make the show. Errors get corrected on the record.
